Application name: Study Sprout
Application description: Study Sprout is a study planner that allows you to see the tasks you need to accomplish. We have two views: one a list where you see all your assignments, and the other is a calendar format so you see when assignments are due. 
Audience: Students (undergrad and postgrad)
Vision Statement: For collegiate students, who need help organizing their tasks. The Study Sprout is a study planner that helps students keep up with assignments. Unlike Notion, our product allows for collaboration on tasks.
